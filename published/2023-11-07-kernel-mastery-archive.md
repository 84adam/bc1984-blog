*Saying goodbye to my former blog: kernelmastery.com.*

I started Kernel Mastery to document my learning especially of Linux, Networking and Programming. I started it in 2015 using an Ubuntu 14 server, with a beta version of the Ghost blogging framework. I have learned a lot since then and have been a software developer officially now for 3 years at this point. So it took me around 5 solid years of studying, coursework and career moves in the right general direction to "arrive" at my chosen destination. I credit this blog project for helping me stay on track and also in establishing my "Proof of Work" over the years.

*Peace~*
