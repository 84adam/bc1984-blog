### Sign up to get an email any time a major security announcement is made for these top Linux and BSD operating systems:

- **Debian,** `debian-security-announce`: <https://lists.debian.org/debian-security-announce>
- **Ubuntu,** `ubuntu-security-announce`: <https://lists.ubuntu.com/mailman/listinfo/ubuntu-security-announce> ; RSS: <https://ubuntu.com/security/notices/rss.xml>
- **Red Hat, CentOS, Rocky Linux, AlmaLinux,** `rhsa-announce`: <https://listman.redhat.com/mailman/listinfo/rhsa-announce>
- **Fedora,** `stack.watch fedora`: <https://stack.watch/product/fedoraproject/>
- **SUSE, openSUSE,** `sle-security-updates`: <https://lists.suse.com/mailman/listinfo/sle-security-updates>
- **FreeBSD,** `freebsd-security`: <https://lists.freebsd.org/subscription/freebsd-security>
- **OpenBSD,** `announce@openbsd.org`: Archive: <https://marc.info/?l=openbsd-announce> ; Email to `majordomo@openbsd.org` with a message body of "`subscribe openbsd-announce`" to subscribe
