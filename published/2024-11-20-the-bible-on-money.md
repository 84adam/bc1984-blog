## What does the bible say about money, wealth and investing?

**Here are a few passages I liked:**

1. Spread out your bets: businesses, land, investments, charities, skill-building, etc.

*Invest in seven ventures, yes, in eight;  
you do not know what disaster may come upon the land.*  
Ecclesiastes 11:2 NIV

2. Be careful with leverage; build in a wide margin of safety when taking on debt

*The rich rule over the poor,  
and the borrower is slave to the lender.*  
Proverbs 22:7 NIV

3. Do your due diligence; reinvest profits; work hard and work smart

*She considers a field and buys it;  
out of her earnings she plants a vineyard.  
She sets about her work vigorously;  
her arms are strong for her tasks.  
She sees that her trading is profitable,  
and her lamp does not go out at night.*  
Proverbs 31:16-18 NIV

4. Live well and always count your blessings

*Honor the Lord with your wealth,  
with the firstfruits of all your crops;  
then your barns will be filled to overflowing,  
and your vats will brim over with new wine.*  
Proverbs 3:9-10 NIV

5. Stay organized and plan for the future

*Suppose one of you wants to build a tower.  
Won’t you first sit down and estimate the cost*  
*to see if you have enough money to complete it?  
For if you lay the foundation and are not able to finish it,*   
*everyone who sees it will ridicule you, saying,*   
*‘This person began to build and wasn’t able to finish.’*  
Luke 14:28-30 NIV

6. Wealth is ephemeral; stay humble and live a good life

*Do not wear yourself out to get rich;  
do not trust your own cleverness.  
Cast but a glance at riches, and they are gone,  
for they will surely sprout wings  
and fly off to the sky like an eagle.*  
Proverbs 23:4-5 NIV

7. Protect what you have; remain vigilant

*Be sure you know the condition of your flocks,  
give careful attention to your herds;  
for riches do not endure forever,  
and a crown is not secure for all generations.*  
Proverbs 27:23-24 NIV
