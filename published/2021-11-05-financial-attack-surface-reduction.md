![Financial Attack Surface Reduction Framework](/images/2021/11/parapet2.JPG)

- **Don't advertise your wealth or income.** Keep this information private.
- Reduce your costs and **maintain a frugal lifestyle**.
- **Keep records of every time you trade a 'sound money asset' (gold, silver, bitcoin).** Collectibles tax or capital gains tax may apply.
- ~ Pay taxes on your gains every year, as required. (Better than going to prison or being audited!)
- ~ Minimize trading and minimize your tax burden.
- ~ Where possible, consider borrowing against your assets as opposed to selling them to cover large expenses.
- **Keep at least half of your assets outside of the traditional financial system.**
- ~ Move bitcoin into cold-storage, multi-signature vaults ([Sparrow](https://www.sparrowwallet.com/)).
- ~ Keep some cash at home.
- ~ Keep some physical [gold](https://silvergoldbull.com/gold#o=priceoz&d=asc) or [silver](https://silvergoldbull.com/silver#o=priceoz&d=asc) bullion at home. And get to know the people at your local coin shop. (Do they pay cash for bullion?)
- **Use non-custodial coinjoin ([samourai](https://samouraiwallet.com/whirlpoolJ), [sparrow](https://sparrowwallet.com/)) to anonymize your bitcoin** (breaking the link to its past history) prior to spending it, and prior to sending into cold-storage.
- **Use a [VPN](https://mullvad.net/en/) or the [Tor Browser](https://www.torproject.org/download/) as much as possible** while interacting with finance apps and websites, or while transacting in or coinjoining your bitcoin. Change your IP address regularly.
- **Run your own Bitcoin Full Node at home ([Raspibolt](https://raspibolt.github.io/raspibolt/)).** Broadcast your transactions via [mempool.space](http://mempoolhqx4isw62xs7abwphsq7ldayuidyx2v2oethdhhj6mlo2r6ad.onion/tx/push) or [blockstream.info](http://explorerzydxu5ecjrkwceayqybizmpjjznk5izmitf2modhcusuqlid.onion/tx/push) Tor Hidden Service where possible.
- **Be Street Smart. Learn Self-Defense.** Learn how to safely use and store a handgun (or larger).
- **Stay Humble.** Keep it simple stupid (KISS).
