Distinct Goals:

- Retire early
- Grow a business
- Live free

## Define Failure First

> "The essence of strategy is choosing what not to do." --Michael Porter

Consider defining what it looks like to *not* achieve your goals first. Then consider all the ways that you can avoid the failure cases. What are the most critical of mistakes to avoid?

1. Retire Early: Failure would mean not having enough money to retire by the target age. This might happen due to unforeseen expenses or overspending/overindulgence.
2. Grow a Business: The business might stagnate or fail. Competitors could overrun you. Poor management could undermine you.
3. Live Free: Failure could entail getting into legal trouble, or getting into debt. Both of these would hinder and trap you.

## Plan for Greatness, and Beyond

> "You've got to think about big things while you're doing small things, so that all the small things go in the right direction." --Alvin Toffler

Plan for Success. Think big.

> "Even if you're on the right track, you'll get run over if you just sit there." --Will Rogers

Think about what you'll want to achieve after meeting your first objective. Visualize yourself visualizing the next major accomplishment.

1. Retire Early: Think about how you'll feel when you get there, and what you'll do with your time.
2. Grow a Business: Imagine the impact you'll have, and how much more of an impact you could have if you continue further down the path.
3. Live Free: Can you see yourself surrounded by like-minded, sovereign individuals in the future? Make it so.

## Act and React

> "No plan survives contact with the enemy." --Correlli Barnett

Be prepared to hit upon obstacles.

> "You have to be fast on your feet and adaptive or else a strategy is useless." --Charles de Gaulle

Adapt to changing circumstances.

1. Retire Early: The market goes south. Certain positions lag or underperform. How can you adjust and compensate to ensure steady progress?
2. Grow a Business: Your founding partner goes in another direction. You hit upon a recession. Think about how you could adapt to keep things running.
3. Live Free: New state income tax proposed. Increased regulatory scrutiny on homeschooling. Do you move, or just modify your tactics and approach?

## The Undisclosed Strategy

> "All men can see these tactics whereby I conquer, but what none can see is the strategy out of which victory is evolved." --Sun Tzu

Announcing your plans makes you less motivated to accomplish them. (See: <https://sive.rs/zipit> )
