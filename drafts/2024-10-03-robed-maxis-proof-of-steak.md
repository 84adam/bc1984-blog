#### What is the life-changing hidden knowledge that we bitcoiners have?

- fiat money is fake
- central banks are evil
- gold is not digital
- only the original digital distributed p2p proof-of-work verified ledger known as bitcoin will serve humanity as money

### NEMESIS

#### Who or what is holding us back as bitcoiners?

- globalists pigdogs, central banksters and keynesian economists
- government lawfare, taxation, corruption and regulatory capture

### ECSTASY

#### What are the typical transformative experiences that new bitcoiners undergo?

- receiving or buying your first sats
- sending bitcoin
- orange pilling: converting another normie from nocoiner to coiner
